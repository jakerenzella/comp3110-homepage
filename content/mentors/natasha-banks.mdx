Tash is Program Director for Day of AI Australia, a free world-class AI literacy program for K-12 teachers and students across Australia. Her mission is to empower young Australians to understand AI, use it responsibly and safely, and confidently shape an AI-powered future. Tash brings over a decade of experience in project management, operations, and stakeholder engagement. She previously served as Head of Operations at Future Women and held roles at the Australian Broadcasting Corporation, including Projects Lead for the Content Ideas Lab.
