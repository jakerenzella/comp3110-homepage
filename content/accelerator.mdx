# The Accelerator

COMP3110 is run like a startup accelerator rather than a typical lecture course. You may join
a small team, have access to industry mentors, identify and pitch a real problem, and move a real machine learning
system from idea to deployment. Lectures will provide industry lectures, AI/ML content, and lessons learned that should help you through your project.

The term is organised into five phases: a two-week **Onboarding**, three **Sprints**,
and **Demo Day**.

## Onboarding: Weeks 1-2

The first two weeks are a foundations sprint. You form teams, pitch a problem, and
stand up the tooling you will use all term. We will explore version control for data and models,
a project tracker, touch on deployment.

By the end of Onboarding every team should have a running (if trivial) end-to-end pipeline.

## Sprint 1: Weeks 3-5

Teams gain access to mentors, scope your problems down, set up pipelines.

## Sprint 2: Weeks 7-9

Improving your metric, typically a focus on data, not the model and focusing on good evals.

## Sprint 3: Week 10

Mostly focused on preparing for your Demo Day.

## Mentors

Every team has access to bookable mentors who can assist with both technical and non-technical problems. More coming soon.

## Demo Day format

The term ends with Demo Day. Each team gives a short, live presentation of a
**deployed** system to an audience of peers, mentors, and industry guests, followed
by questions.
