# The Accelerator

COMP3110 is run like a startup accelerator rather than a typical lecture course. You may join
a small team, have access to industry mentors, identify and pitch a real problem, and move a real machine learning
system from idea to deployment. Lectures will provide industry lectures, AI/ML content, and lessons learned that should help you through your project.

The term is organised into three phases: a two-week **Onboarding**, three **Sprints**,
and **Demo Day**.

## Mentors

Every team has access to bookable mentors who can assist with both technical and non-technical problems. Details are found on the course forum, EdStem.

## Demo Day format

Demo Day is a short, in-person presentation of your Accelerator project to an audience of peers, mentors, and industry guests.
