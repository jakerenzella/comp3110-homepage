## The problem

A regional distribution partner over-stocked slow items and ran out of fast ones.
The team built a demand forecaster to drive smarter reorder points across
thousands of SKUs.

## What they built

A hierarchical forecasting system reconciles predictions across product, category,
and warehouse levels so the numbers add up, then feeds reorder recommendations to
a planning dashboard.

## Engineering highlights

- **Hierarchical reconciliation** so store-level and category-level forecasts are
  mutually consistent.
- **A reproducible training pipeline** with data validation gates that block bad
  batches before they reach the model.
- **Backtesting harness** that simulates the reorder policy on held-out history.

## What they'd do next

Add promotions and supplier lead-time variability as first-class features.
