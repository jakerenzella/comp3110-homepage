## The problem

UNSW's campus draws megawatts of power with a daily and seasonal rhythm. Better
day-ahead forecasts let facilities pre-cool buildings and shift load off peak
tariffs. The team built a forecaster that facilities staff can actually act on.

## What they built

A probabilistic time-series model ingests weather forecasts, timetable occupancy,
and historical meter data to predict per-building demand with uncertainty bands.
Forecasts are published to a dashboard and refreshed hourly.

## Engineering highlights

- **Quantile forecasts** rather than point estimates, so operators see the risk
  range, not a single misleadingly precise number.
- **Backfilled feature store** so training and serving read features the same way.
- **A retraining schedule** that automatically re-fits as new meter data lands.

## What they'd do next

Close the loop by feeding the forecasts into the building management system for
automated pre-cooling.
