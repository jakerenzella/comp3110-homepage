## The problem

Fire agencies need to know where risk is concentrating days ahead. The team built
a model that fuses satellite imagery and weather to map short-term wildfire risk
across a region.

## What they built

A geospatial pipeline pulls multispectral satellite tiles and gridded weather,
computes vegetation-dryness features, and predicts a risk surface that renders as
an interactive map layer.

## Engineering highlights

- **Tiled inference** over large satellite scenes so the system scales to a whole
  region without running out of memory.
- **Calibrated risk scores** validated against historical fire records.
- **A reproducible geospatial feature pipeline** with versioned inputs.

## What they'd do next

Incorporate live sensor feeds and push alerts to agency dashboards.
