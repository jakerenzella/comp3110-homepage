## The problem

Real-time recognition of Auslan (Australian Sign Language) can make everyday
interactions more accessible — but only if it runs on a phone, offline, without
sending video to a server. The team took on the constraints as the point.

## What they built

A lightweight pose-estimation front end feeds a temporal model that recognises a
vocabulary of common signs on-device. The whole pipeline runs on a mid-range
phone with no network connection.

## Engineering highlights

- **On-device inference** with a quantised model, keeping video off the network
  entirely for privacy.
- **A data-collection app** built with community input to gather signing samples
  ethically and with consent.
- **Latency budgeting** across pose extraction and classification to stay real-time.

## What they'd do next

Expand the vocabulary and work with Deaf community groups on evaluation.
