## The problem

Clinicians spend hours turning free-text consultation notes into structured
discharge summaries. The team built a system that drafts a summary a clinician
can review and correct, rather than one that replaces them.

## What they built

A retrieval-augmented pipeline grounds a language model in the patient's own
notes and a controlled medical vocabulary, then produces a structured draft with
every claim linked back to a source sentence. A clinician's edits are captured as
preference data to improve later drafts.

## Engineering highlights

- **Citation-grounded generation** — every line in the summary points to the note
  it came from, so reviewers can verify quickly.
- **A domain evaluation set** built with a clinical advisor, scoring factuality and
  omission rather than fluency.
- **Human-review UI** that measures edit distance as a live quality signal.

## What they'd do next

Fine-tune on the collected clinician edits and add specialty-specific templates.
